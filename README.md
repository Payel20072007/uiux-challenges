🎨 UI/UX Learning Hub

Welcome to the UI/UX Learning Hub!

This is our collaborative space for learning, experimenting, creating, and growing together as UI/UX designers.

Through practical design challenges, we aim to improve our design thinking, Figma skills, creativity, documentation, and GitHub collaboration skills.

This is not just about completing assignments. It's about learning from each other and becoming better designers together.

🚀 What We Do Here
Learn UI/UX design principles and practices.
Explore Figma and its design capabilities.
Participate in weekly design challenges.
Practice Git and GitHub through real submissions.
Share design ideas and creative solutions.
Learn from each other's work and feedback.

🎯 How to Participate

Follow these steps for every design challenge:

Read the challenge brief carefully.
Understand the requirements and expected outcome.
Create your design in Figma.
Fork this repository.
Create a new branch using your name and challenge number.
Add your design screenshots and project documentation.
Include your Figma prototype link in your README.
Commit and push your changes.
Open a Pull Request to submit your work.
Wait for feedback and review.
📋 Submission Guidelines

Every submission should include:

Your name or GitHub username.
Challenge number and title.
Figma design link.
Design screenshots.
A short explanation of your design.
Key design decisions or learnings.

🤝 Community Guidelines
Respect everyone's creative ideas.
Give constructive feedback.
Do not copy another member's work.
Do not modify another member's submission.
Keep your Figma links and documentation organized.
Be open to learning and experimenting.
Support each other throughout the journey.
