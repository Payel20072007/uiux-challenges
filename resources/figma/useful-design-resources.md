You don't need to use everything. Explore, experiment and find what works best for you!

# A Small Reminder #
Don't just collect references. Try to understand:
- Why does this design work?
- How is the information organized?
- What makes the interface easy to use?
- How are colors, typography and spacing used?
- What would you improve?

Observe. Explore. Experiment. Create.

Happy designing! 💜
