📤 Design Challenge – Submission Guide

Welcome!

This guide explains how to submit your Figma design through our GitHub organization.

Please follow the same submission process for every challenge so that our projects remain organized.

Step 1 – Check the Challenge

Visit the challenges folder in our repository.

Open the relevant challenge folder and read its README.md.

Understand:

What you need to design.
The design requirements.
The expected deliverables.
The submission deadline.
Step 2 – Create Your Design in Figma

Complete the assigned design in Figma.

Make sure your work is organized and presentable.

Before submitting:

Check your typography.
Review spacing and alignment.
Organize your layers.
Check your components and Auto Layout.
Make sure your prototype works, if required.
Step 3 – Fork the Repository

Open our organization's uiux-challenges repository.

Click the Fork option to create your own copy.

You will now have a copy of the repository under your GitHub account.

Step 4 – Create Your Branch

Create a separate branch for your submission.

Use this naming format:

your-name/challenge-01

Example:

rahul/challenge-01

This keeps your work separate from the main repository.

Step 5 – Add Your Submission

Inside the submissions folder, create your own folder.

Follow this structure:

text
submissions/
└── your-name/
    └── challenge-01/
        ├── README.md
        ├── preview.png
        └── screens/
            ├── screen-01.png
            └── screen-02.png


Your submission should contain:

A Figma design or prototype link.
A preview screenshot.
Additional design screenshots, if required.
A short explanation of your design.
Step 6 – Write Your Submission README

Use the following format:

markdown
# Challenge 01 – Login Page Design

**Name:** Your Name

**GitHub Username:** your-username

## Figma Link
Paste your Figma link here.

## Design Overview
Briefly describe your design.

## Design Decisions
Explain your choice of colors, typography,
layout and components.

## What I Learned
Mention something new you explored.

Step 7 – Commit Your Changes

Save your work with a meaningful commit message.

Example:

Add Rahul's Challenge 01 submission

Step 8 – Push Your Changes

Push your branch to your GitHub fork.

Step 9 – Create a Pull Request

Open your fork on GitHub.

Click Contribute → Open pull request (or the equivalent Pull Request option).

Make sure the destination is the original organization's repository and its main branch.

Use this title format:

[Challenge 01] Your Name – Login Page

Add a short description of your submission.

Finally, click Create pull request.

Step 10 – Wait for Review

Your submission will be reviewed by the UI/UX Lead.

You may receive suggestions or feedback.

If changes are requested, update your branch and push the changes. They will appear in the same Pull Request.

⚠️ Important Guidelines
Submit your own original design work.
Do not copy another member's submission.
Do not modify another member's folder.
Use the correct folder and branch naming format.
Check that your Figma link works for authorized reviewers.
Keep your submission files organized.
Do not upload unnecessarily large files.
💜 Final Note

The goal isn't just to submit an assignment.

It's to learn how to present your work, document your design decisions and collaborate with others.

Happy designing! 🚀
